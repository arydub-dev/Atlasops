# ATLASOPS product overview

ATLASOPS combines shipment, inventory, warehouse and supplier records into operational visibility, explainable risk scores, alerts and decision support. It complements systems of record. Recommendations do not execute supply-chain changes.

Pilot scope: one organization, one primary data source, CSV/XLSX imports, Mission Control, exception review and a weekly executive brief. Salesforce, Dynamics BC and UPS remain beta until each customer's sandbox and production permissions are verified. SAP, Oracle, NetSuite and arbitrary WMS/TMS integrations are not promised as working connectors.

Demo data is synthetic. Simulation results are conditional estimates, not guaranteed outcomes. Copilot is grounded in authorized context; generated text still needs human review. See AI_COPILOT.md.

Current launch decision is in LAUNCH_REPORT.md. No uptime, certification, scale or ROI claim follows from feature availability.
