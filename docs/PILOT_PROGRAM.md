# Controlled pilot program

Initial ICP hypothesis: a distributor or small manufacturer operating multiple warehouses and suppliers, with recurring shipment/inventory exceptions and usable CSV/XLSX exports. Prioritize available data and a committed operations sponsor over logo size. Enterprise integration requirements outside verified connectors need separate scoping.

Proposed duration: four weeks, after production-like staging gates pass.

Week 1: scope one workflow, record baseline, approve data access, import a sample and reconcile totals.
Week 2: review daily exceptions and record which alerts are useful, noisy or missing.
Week 3: use supplier/inventory analysis, Copilot and one explicit simulation; record actions humans chose.
Week 4: compare outcomes, collect user feedback, review reliability and decide whether to convert.

Success criteria agreed before start: source reconciliation accepted; users can locate priority exceptions; recurring brief takes less measured staff time; actionable findings verified by customer; no unresolved P0/P1 incidents. Do not invent savings or imply avoided losses without customer evidence.

Weekly review: sponsor, data owner and operator review activation, data quality, false alerts, time to insight, defects and next actions. Maintain an issue register with owners/dates.

Pilot-to-paid: agree plan, configured price, data scope, support, signed terms/privacy obligations and payment method. Validate billing and recovery before invoicing. A pilot participant is not a testimonial or public reference without permission. Customer recruitment and onboarding are NOT EXECUTED here.
