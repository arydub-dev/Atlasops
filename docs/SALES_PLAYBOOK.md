# Sales playbook

## 30-second pitch

ATLASOPS brings shipment, inventory, supplier and risk data into one operational view. Your team can spot exceptions, investigate dependencies and review recommended actions without assembling the picture from separate exports.

## Two-minute pitch

Supply-chain teams often reconcile shipment, stock and supplier data manually. ATLASOPS organizes that data into Mission Control and focused operational views, then supports risk review, conditional simulations and executive briefs. It complements the ERP rather than replacing your system of record. Start with one source and one measurable workflow. Beta connectors and generated recommendations need validation against your data.

## Discovery

Where do shipment and inventory records live? Who owns data quality? How are exceptions prioritized? How long does a briefing take? Which supplier failures matter most? What would demonstrate useful value in four weeks? Which security/integration requirements are mandatory?

## 15–20 minute demo

Minutes 0–3: agree on the operational question and label every record synthetic.
3–7: open a delayed shipment; inspect warehouse stock and dependencies.
7–11: review deteriorating supplier reliability and affected records.
11–14: investigate approaching stockout and replenishment assumptions.
14–17: request an executive brief and inspect its context; run a scenario with explicit inputs.
17–20: discuss data availability, a scoped pilot and success criteria.

Use the existing synthetic seed engine only in an isolated demo database. Do not populate a production organization with sample records or claim demonstration relationships prove a customer's integration.

## Objections

“We have an ERP”: ATLASOPS complements operational records with a shared exception-review workflow.
“Another dashboard?”: demonstrate an actionable exception and investigation, not chart count.
“We can build it”: compare maintenance/data reconciliation effort using the prospect's own estimates.
“Will it integrate?”: CSV/XLSX now; verify beta Salesforce/Dynamics BC/UPS in their sandbox. Other connectors are planned.
“Is it secure?”: describe implemented permission and isolation controls and show actual test evidence; no certification claim.
“Can it scale?”: share measured workload results only, not unsupported user counts.
“How does AI work?”: authorized context and optional model provider; humans verify recommendations.
